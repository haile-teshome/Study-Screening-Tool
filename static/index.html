📋 Study Guidelines

SCREENING CRITERIA

Include:

  • Primary research on plasma phosphorylated tau biomarkers (p-tau181, p-tau217, and/or p-tau231)
  • Reports diagnostic accuracy metrics (AUC, sensitivity, specificity, or predictive values) against an established reference standard (amyloid PET, tau PET, CSF biomarkers, or neuropathology)
  • Human adult participants (mean age ≥18; mixed samples acceptable if ≥80% adult)
  • Sample size n ≥ 30
  • Published in peer-reviewed journal between 2018–present
  • Full text available in English

Exclude:

  • Review articles, meta-analyses, editorials, commentaries, letters without original data
  • Conference abstracts without subsequent full publication
  • Preprints not yet peer-reviewed
  • Animal or in vitro studies only
  • Studies in exclusively pediatric populations (mean age <18)
  • Studies measuring only CSF or imaging tau without plasma p-tau as primary biomarker
  • Case reports or case series (n<30)

PICO EXTRACTION

Population (P):

  • Extract: Age range, diagnosis, sample size
  • Example: Cognitively normal adults, n=304

Intervention (I):

  • Extract: Plasma p-tau assay/method used (specify variant: p-tau181/217/231)
  • Example: Lumipulse plasma p-tau217

Comparator (C):

  • Extract: Reference standard (PET, CSF, neuropathology)
  • Example: CSF Aβ42/40 ratio

Outcome (O):

  • Extract: Primary diagnostic metrics with 95% CI when reported
  • Example: AUC 0.92 (95% CI 0.89-0.95)
